# Human Handoff and Sales Escalation

The B2B assistant was designed to support the commercial team, not replace it.

Its role was to handle the conversational work that could be automated before salesperson involvement:

- answer brand questions
- answer technical product questions
- recommend relevant product context
- deliver commercial and educational material
- identify professional B2B intent
- progressively collect qualification information
- persist that information across messages
- prepare structured commercial context
- escalate the opportunity when human involvement became appropriate

The final commercial relationship and closing process remained human.

```text
Acquisition
    ↓
WhatsApp conversation
    ↓
Product education
    ↓
Commercial intent
    ↓
Qualification
    ↓
Persistent lead state
    ↓
Operational CRM
    ↓
Human validation
    ↓
Sales follow-up
```

## Why the handoff existed

The sales team operated beyond WhatsApp.

Representatives also performed commercial follow-up and in-person activity with professional salons, which meant incoming digital conversations could not depend entirely on a salesperson being immediately available to answer every question manually.

The automation acted as a preparation layer.

Instead of sending every incoming prospect directly to a salesperson:

```text
Ad
    ↓
WhatsApp
    ↓
Salesperson must answer everything manually
```

the system could perform:

```text
Ad
    ↓
WhatsApp
    ↓
AI product / brand conversation
    ↓
Progressive qualification
    ↓
Structured commercial context
    ↓
Salesperson receives a better-prepared opportunity
```

The objective was therefore not to eliminate the salesperson.

It was to reduce repetitive work before salesperson involvement and allow human commercial time to be used where it had greater value.

## Commercial escalation signals

Human handoff could become relevant when the conversation showed stronger commercial intent.

Examples include situations such as:

```text
"I want to work with Davines"

"I want to speak with sales"

"Can someone visit my salon?"

"I want more information about becoming a client"
```

These signals are different from a purely technical question such as:

```text
"What do you recommend for frizz?"
```

The latter can remain in product exploration.

The former can activate the structured qualification path.

```text
Product / technical interest
        ↓
Continue conversation

Strong commercial intent
        ↓
APOSTOLADO
        ↓
Qualification
        ↓
Human handoff
```

## Qualification before handoff

The workflow attempts to collect useful commercial context before completing the handoff.

Persisted information can include:

```text
contacto

salon
ciudad
distrito
estilistas
telefono
correo
marca
driver
horario
lineas
```

The information is collected progressively rather than through a single rigid form.

For example:

```text
Prospect identifies as salon owner
        ↓
tipo = salon

Mentions Lima
        ↓
ciudad = Lima

Provides district
        ↓
distrito persisted

Explains current professional brand
        ↓
marca persisted

Explains commercial motivation
        ↓
driver persisted
```

Previously captured fields can be reused so the assistant does not need to ask the same question again.

## Handoff as persistent application state

Human escalation is not represented only by a sentence generated by the AI.

The system persists handoff state in Supabase.

Three important fields are:

```text
estado = HUMANO
asesor_solicitado = true
bot_pausado = true
```

The production persistence logic checks whether the assistant has closed the interaction toward human follow-up.

It also checks whether the conversation had already been handed off.

Conceptually:

```text
Current response
        ↓
Handoff detected?
        │
        ├── YES
        │     ↓
        │ bot_pausado = true
        │ asesor_solicitado = true
        │ estado = HUMANO
        │
        └── NO
              ↓
        Preserve previous handoff state
        if one already exists
```

This means handoff survives subsequent webhook executions.

It is not dependent on the language model remembering that a salesperson had already been requested.

## Observed production handoff state

The analyzed Supabase snapshot contained **61 sessions** with:

```text
estado = HUMANO
```

The same 61 sessions also had:

```text
asesor_solicitado = true
bot_pausado = true
```

This is a strong observed state invariant in the production snapshot.

The 61 sessions were distributed as:

| fase_actual | estado | Sessions |
|---|---|---:|
| captura | HUMANO | 35 |
| exploracion | HUMANO | 26 |
| **Total** | **HUMANO** | **61** |

The second group shows that not every production handoff finished with `fase_actual = captura`.

This is documented as an observed production behavior rather than forcing the data into an idealized state machine.

## Handoff detection in the persistence layer

The production workflow contains explicit logic for detecting language indicating that the prospect is moving to human follow-up.

Examples of detected patterns include variations conceptually equivalent to:

```text
our advisor will contact you
our team will contact you
an advisor will write to you
we will contact you by WhatsApp
```

The persistence layer then combines the new result with previous Supabase state.

Conceptually:

```text
botPausado = handoff detected from current response

yaEraPausado =
    previous bot_pausado
    OR previous asesor_solicitado
    OR previous estado = HUMANO

botPausadoFinal =
    botPausado
    OR yaEraPausado
```

This is important because human ownership should not disappear simply because a later AI response does not repeat the handoff phrase.

## State preservation after handoff

Once a conversation has been escalated, previously captured commercial information is preserved.

The system should not behave like:

```text
Human handoff completed
        ↓
Customer sends "gracias"
        ↓
Qualification starts again
```

Instead:

```text
Human handoff completed
        ↓
Persistent HUMANO state
        ↓
Customer sends another message
        ↓
Router reads previous state
        ↓
Existing qualification remains preserved
```

This prevents the commercial journey from being restarted accidentally.

## Post-handoff conversation

A handoff does not necessarily mean the customer stops using the WhatsApp channel.

A prospect may still ask another product or technical question after being referred to sales.

The routing architecture therefore recognizes post-handoff state separately from initial qualification.

```text
estado = HUMANO
        OR
asesor_solicitado = true
        OR
bot_pausado = true
        ↓
Post-handoff context
```

The important behavior is that a new message should not erase the previous commercial state or restart the prospect as if they were completely new.

This distinction was refined during production testing because post-handoff behavior created edge cases in earlier workflow versions.

## Human review before sales follow-up

The operational CRM included an intentional human-in-the-loop layer.

The marketing team reviewed B2B records before they were passed forward for commercial action.

The real process was closer to:

```text
Bot conversation
        ↓
Structured information
        ↓
CRM
        ↓
Marketing review
        ↓
Is this genuinely a B2B opportunity?
        ↓
Correct / complete minor information if necessary
        ↓
Prepare for commercial team
        ↓
Sales follow-up
```

Humans could therefore identify situations where:

```text
information was incomplete
a small field needed correction
the prospect was not actually B2B
commercial context needed interpretation
```

The system automated repetitive work without removing commercial judgment.

## Sales-team distribution

The commercial team operated with different geographic responsibilities.

Once the opportunity had sufficient context, it could be passed to the appropriate commercial follow-up process according to factors such as location and territory.

Relevant location fields included:

```text
ciudad
distrito
```

This mattered because salesperson activity could involve visiting the professional client rather than only continuing a digital conversation.

The automated conversation could therefore prepare information before the physical commercial interaction.

```text
Prospect learns about Davines
        ↓
Product needs identified
        ↓
Commercial information collected
        ↓
Location known
        ↓
Sales team receives context
        ↓
Relevant representative follows up
```

## Human handoff vs autonomous closing

The architecture deliberately stops short of describing the AI as the entity responsible for the final sale.

Revenue outcomes depend on multiple layers:

```text
Paid acquisition
+
Creative
+
Conversational automation
+
Qualification
+
Human validation
+
Sales execution
```

The AI system contributed by improving the path between acquisition and commercial follow-up.

The salesperson remained responsible for the final commercial relationship and close.

## Evolution of the handoff design

Human escalation changed considerably during development.

Earlier project versions already included the concept of:

```text
lead capture
CRM registration
advisor alert
human escalation
```

Production testing then exposed additional problems that required more explicit state management.

Examples included:

```text
bot continuing or stopping incorrectly after handoff
handoff state being lost between messages
qualification restarting
duplicate advisor behavior
previously captured data being overwritten
```

These issues motivated stronger persistent-state rules around:

```text
bot_pausado
asesor_solicitado
estado = HUMANO
```

The final architecture therefore reflects iteration based on observed conversational behavior rather than only the original design.

## Anti-duplicate handoff principle

A mature handoff flow must distinguish between:

```text
First transition to human ownership
```

and:

```text
A conversation that was already handed off
```

Conceptually:

```text
Was the conversation already HUMANO?
        │
        ├── NO
        │     ↓
        │ New handoff
        │
        └── YES
              ↓
        Preserve existing handoff
        Do not treat it as a brand-new lead
```

This prevents repeated user messages after escalation from being interpreted as entirely new commercial opportunities.

## Why human-in-the-loop was appropriate

The objective was not maximum automation.

The objective was useful automation.

AI is effective at:

```text
answering repetitive questions
explaining product information
interpreting natural language
collecting structured information
maintaining conversation
preparing context
```

Humans remain valuable for:

```text
commercial judgment
relationship building
territory management
negotiation
in-person follow-up
closing
```

The system was designed around this division of responsibilities.

## Engineering lesson

The main lesson from the handoff layer is that the boundary between automation and humans must itself be engineered.

It is not enough for the AI to say:

```text
"An advisor will contact you."
```

The application also needs to know:

```text
Was the handoff actually completed?
Has this user already been handed off?
Should qualification continue?
Should previously captured data remain?
Should this interaction create another commercial action?
Who now owns the conversation?
```

Persistent state turns a conversational promise into an operational transition.

That distinction is what makes the handoff part of the application architecture rather than only part of the chatbot copy.
