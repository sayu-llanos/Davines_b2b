# Results

The B2B acquisition and WhatsApp sales channel showed measurable commercial performance during the project period.

## High-performance campaign period

- ROAS: approximately 41x
- Meta Ads spend: S/836
- Attributed sales: S/34,571
- New commercial openings increased from approximately 3 to 10 in one month
- Lead qualification time was reduced from hours to minutes

## Broader B2B performance — May to July 2026

Across the broader May–July period, the B2B acquisition channel operating alongside the automation recorded:

- 878 B2B conversations
- 18 new account openings
- S/57.9K in sales
- S/2.35K in Meta Ads spend
- 24.7x blended ROAS

## Attribution note

These metrics reflect the performance of the B2B acquisition and WhatsApp sales channel during the periods analyzed.

They should not be attributed exclusively to the AI assistant, since results also depended on advertising performance, commercial follow-up, offer quality and sales execution.

My contribution focused on the automation and conversational system supporting the WhatsApp journey, including intent routing, lead qualification, product guidance, persistent conversation state, CRM integration and human handoff.
